5PC® is a pop culture compendium. Five pillars: film, series, books, games, music.

The idea is simple. Instead of reviewing one thing in isolation, I look at how the same story or franchise lives across different media. A Bond film, a Bond novel, a Bond game, a Bond theme song. They are all telling the same story in different languages. This site is where I try to read them together.

I built this because I am often curious about a franchise. Why does it last? What makes people keep coming back? I am also a collector by temperament, but I cannot afford to collect expensive things. A website is cheaper. So this is my collection.

I write everything myself. No team. No sponsorships. No chasing release dates. When I write about something, it is because I spent time with it.

This is a personal project, not a publication. Updates are irregular. Some posts are long. Some are short. The one rule is that I only write about what I actually finished or experienced. No hot takes from trailers.
