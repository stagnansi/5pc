007 First Light is an action-adventure game from IO Interactive, the
Danish studio behind the Hitman series. It is a James Bond origin
story and the first major Bond game in over a decade. You play as a
26-year-old James Bond, a Royal Navy air crewman who is talented,
promising, and frequently reckless. He is not yet the man in the
tuxedo. He has no licence to kill, no Aston Martin, and no vodka
martini. He is a recruit trying to earn his place.

The game is played from a third-person perspective and blends stealth,
investigation, hand-to-hand combat, and gunplay. It is entirely
single-player. IO Interactive brought its experience from Hitman,
where you study a location, find a way in, and choose how to handle
each obstacle. In First Light, you can go silent or go loud. You can
fight with fists or firepower, use gadgets to infiltrate, or bluff
your way past guards. The approach is up to you.

## For Newcomers

You do not need any Bond background to follow this story. The game
assumes you are meeting James Bond at the same time the people around
him are, as someone unproven. If you have seen any Bond film, you will
recognise M, Q, and Moneypenny. Here they are younger and still
figuring out what they will become. The cast includes Priyanga
Burford as M, Alastair McKenzie as Q, Kiera Lester as Moneypenny, and
Lennie James as John Greenway, Bond's reluctant mentor. New characters
include Gemma Chan as Dr. Selina Tan, Lenny Kravitz as the Pirate King
Bawma, and Noemie Nakai as the mysterious Miss Roth.

## The Story

The story begins with Bond as a rookie air crewman on a covert mission
to Iceland. The plane is shot down. Bond is the only survivor. MI6
takes notice and taps him to retrieve a mysterious asset from a remote
island base. Shortly after, he is summoned to join MI6's newly
resurrected 00 program. M sees something in him, even if his
instructors do not.

Bond's first major assignment is to hunt down Agent 009, a former
British agent who has gone rogue. The mission ends in tragedy. Bond
is forced to work with Greenway, who is sceptical of him, to expose a
deep conspiracy and stop a coup at the heart of the state. The
conspiracy revolves around THEIA, a quantum supercomputer that uses
artificial intelligence to track criminal syndicates and direct MI6
agents in the field. THEIA is supposed to be MI6's greatest tool. It
is flawed, compromised, and has been sending agents on misguided
missions for years. Its creator, billionaire Sir Nicholas Webb, has
been covering up its failures and using the technology for his own
gain.

Bond's investigation takes him across the world. He crosses paths
with Isola Vale, a woman whose identity and intentions remain unclear
even to MI6. In the end, Isola betrays Bond, kills Webb, and steals
the core of Hyperion, a mirror version of THEIA. She escapes. The
game ends with M granting Bond the 007 codename. The gunbarrel
sequence plays. "James Bond will return."
